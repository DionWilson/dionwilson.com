Duration: Full Day (8-9 hours)

  • Stellenbosch: Historic oak-lined streets, Cape Dutch architecture (45 min from Cape Town)
  • Franschhoek: French Huguenot heritage, gourmet food scene, scenic valley
  • Paarl: Dramatic granite peaks, some of South Africa's oldest vineyards

Constantia Wine Route (Local Option)

For the Constantia property guests - walk or short drive to world-class estates:

  • Groot Constantia: South Africa's oldest wine estate (350+ years)
  • Klein Constantia: Famous for Vin de Constance dessert wine
  • Buitenverwachting: Fine dining + wine tasting
  • Beau Constantia: Stunning views, Chef's Warehouse restaurant

🍽️ Top Restaurants to Book

South Africa does not have a Michelin restaurant guide. The local gold standard is the Eat Out Woolworths Restaurant Awards 2026 (announced March 2026). Cape Town took most of the stars. Salon in Woodstock won three stars and then closed in March 2026, so it is not on this list.

How to use this with 19–21 people: Tasting-menu restaurants seat small parties and book out in December. Plan two kinds of dinners: one or two everyone-together nights, and several split nights of about 8–12 at the starred restaurants. Book the starred tables now.

Book these first (three Eat Out stars)

Salsify at the Roundhouse Above Camps Bay Closest three-star table to the Fresnaye and Camps Bay houses. Historic lodge, Atlantic views, seasonal tasting menu. Lunch is the view. World's 50 Best list. Adults, 8–12. Book lunch.
La Colombe Silvermist, Constantia Nek Closest three-star table to both Constantia houses. French-Asian “food as theatre” over the vineyards. Adults, 8–12.

La Petite Colombe Franschhoek Three stars in the winelands. Pair with the wine-country day rather than a Cape Town night. Wine-day lunch.

Near the Fresnaye and Camps Bay houses

  • Salsify at the Roundhouse ★★★ — Roundhouse Road, off Kloof Road. 021 010 6444, reservations@salsify.co.za. Lunch Tuesday–Saturday from 12:30; dinner from 6:30. A shorter lunch menu is available.

📋 Practical Information

Transportation

  • Recommended: Rent 3-4 vehicles to transport 19+ guests
  • Uber: Available throughout Cape Town, good for smaller groups
  • Private Driver: Consider hiring for wine tours (no drinking and driving)
  • Airport: Cape Town International (CPT) - 20-30 min to properties

Weather (Late December - Early January)

  • Season: Summer in South Africa!
  • Temperature: 20-28°C (68-82°F) average
  • Sun: Sets around 8:00 PM - long evenings
  • Wind: "Cape Doctor" (Southeast wind) common - can be strong
  • Rain: Generally dry, but occasional summer showers

What to Pack

  • Sunscreen (SPF 50+) - Southern Hemisphere sun is strong
  • Sunglasses and hats
  • Light layers (evenings can be cool)
  • Windbreaker for windy beach days
  • Comfortable walking shoes for Robben Island, Table Mountain
  • Smart casual attire for restaurant dinners
  • Swimwear (multiple pools and beaches!)

Safety Tips

  • Cape Town is generally safe in tourist areas
  • Don't leave valuables visible in parked cars
  • Stick to well-traveled areas at night
  • Use official taxis or Uber
  • Keep copies of passports separate from originals

Currency & Payments

  • Currency: South African Rand (ZAR)
  • Exchange Rate: ~R18 = $1 USD (verify before travel)
  • Cards: Visa/Mastercard widely accepted
  • Cash: Useful for tips, markets, small vendors
  • Tipping: 10-15% at restaurants is customary
